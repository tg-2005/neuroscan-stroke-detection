Process

How It Works

Four simple steps from brain scan upload to clinical-grade stroke detection with Grad-CAM visualization.

01
📤
Upload Brain Scan

Upload your CT or MRI neuroimage (DICOM, JPG, PNG). Select the modality — the correct model is automatically chosen.

02
🔬
Deep Learning Analysis

3-Model Ensemble (GoogLeNet + InceptionV3 + Hybrid CNN-ViT) runs on CT; Hybrid CNN-Transformer on MRI. Image is preprocessed and passed through trained convolutional and attention layers.

03
🎯
Stroke Classification

The model classifies: Normal, Ischemic, or Hemorrhagic — with a confidence score for clinical decision-making.

04
🗺️
Grad-CAM Heatmap

A Grad-CAM heatmap overlay highlights the precise brain region driving the prediction for both CT and MRI scans.

Real-World Impact

Why This Matters

Early stroke detection is a matter of life and death — deep learning can help doctors everywhere.

❤️
Early Detection Saves Lives

Stroke is the second leading cause of death worldwide. Detecting it within the golden hour dramatically reduces mortality and long-term disability. Automated screening shortens the critical decision window.

1 in 4
people will experience a stroke in their lifetime
🩺
Assists Doctors in Diagnosis

Radiologists analyze hundreds of neuroimages daily. Our deep learning system acts as an intelligent second opinion — flagging high-confidence positives for immediate review and reducing diagnostic error rates.

40%
reduction in missed diagnoses with AI-assisted tools
🌾
Vital in Rural Areas

Rural hospitals often lack dedicated neuro-radiologists. This system enables general practitioners to obtain immediate screening results, bridging the specialist gap in underserved regions.

65%
of stroke deaths occur in low and middle-income regions
About the Project

About Us

We are a team of innovators focused on transforming healthcare through advanced technology. Our project, Early Stroke Detection Using Deep Learning and Neuroimage Interpretation, is designed to assist in identifying brain stroke conditions quickly and accurately using medical imaging.

Our goal is to provide scalable, intelligent solutions that support early diagnosis and improve patient outcomes in critical situations.

🎯 Our Mission

At our core, we aim to simplify and enhance the process of stroke detection by converting complex neuroimage data into meaningful insights.

We strive to build a system that improves diagnostic efficiency, reduces response time, and supports medical professionals in making informed decisions.

By integrating deep learning with medical imaging, we envision a future where early detection becomes faster, more reliable, and widely accessible.
